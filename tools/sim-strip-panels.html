every kind — the quick row the strip builds for it

One fixed frame per kind: every toggle OFF, no picker, no settings panel, badge = the demo drawing's name. Cells, their order and the faces come from DrawStripAllSlotAt + DrawStripIconRes. The demo drawing wears a blue border and an amber interior (palette swatches 4 and 1), because the MERGED colour cell is a ring of the border's colour around the interior's — one colour would not show it. A kind with no interior shows the ring alone, the plate tone inside.
