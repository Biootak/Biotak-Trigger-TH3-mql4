Same owner, same measured bands — a different face. Nothing here needs a second redesign later.

  • Families are words. NEUTRAL · RED · ORANGE · AMBER · GREEN are captions with a hairline, not a hunt through 64 anonymous squares.
  • Quiet depth. 1px hairlines, a 16px radius, one soft shadow, an inset top highlight on every swatch — depth from three values, not from borders.
  • The studio is a mode, not a fixture. Closed it costs nothing; open it gives a hue rail, an SV square and a live preview with its own hex.
  • Recent has a state. Empty slots are visibly empty rather than blank — the band the old build painted and could not fill.
  • One accent. Amber is reserved for «you are here»: the selected swatch, the active tab, the slider fill. Nothing else competes.
  • Height is still measured. Adding the studio grew the board by exactly its own height — no constant to re-derive.
