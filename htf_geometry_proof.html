HTF candle geometry — P-UI-68

Drawn from the SAME index-space maths HTFCandleGeometry() uses in Biotak/HTFCandles.mqh: the BODY spans the period minus half of SHADOW GAP on each side, and the SHADOW is a filled box of SHADOW WIDTH centred on the body as drawn (a weekend inside one candle is ONE bar slot, so the calendar middle is not the drawn middle). One slot = one HTF period, and the free space between two bodies is the gap you set.
