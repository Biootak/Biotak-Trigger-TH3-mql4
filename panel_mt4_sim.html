Panel simulator — the cards as MetaTrader draws them

Every bitmap below is the real file from Files/Icons; every coordinate is parsed out of Biotak/BiotakPanels.mqh. No terminal involved — this is the pixel layout MT4 will blit, so the baked soft shadows, gradients and radii show up exactly as shipped.

Text is the one approximation. Captions are rendered by the browser at the same em box (the MQL's DPI-compensated point size → 12 px) and the same anchor it sets, but MT4 has its own font stack. Treat the chrome as exact and the type as very close. Every caption that touches a control is measured, never guessed (P-UI-30): label widths come from Arial Bold's real advances at 144 dpi, and a caption with no room left is clipped with .. the way the preview's ellipsis clips it.
